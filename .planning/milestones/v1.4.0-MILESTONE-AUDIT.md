### Gap: OCML-04 Scope Control

**Issue:** `odoc.qa.md` Question 2 captures the user's .mli enrichment scope choice, but `setup/SKILL.md` routes framework Q&A answers in-memory to the researcher only — they are NOT written to `config.yaml`. `doc-author.md` line 372 expects to read the scope from `config.yaml` under "odoc-specific settings", but no such field exists in `config-template.yaml`.

**Impact:** The .mli enrichment workflow's scope gate has no reliable signal across sessions. The enrichment may always run or always skip, depending on how the agent resolves the ambiguous instruction.

**Fix:** Add `odoc_author_scope` field to `config-template.yaml`, update `setup/SKILL.md` Step 3 to persist the Q&A answer, clarify `doc-author.md` field path.

**Affected files:**
- `doc-writer/skills/setup/defaults/config-template.yaml`
- `doc-writer/skills/setup/SKILL.md`
- `doc-writer/agents/doc-author.md`
